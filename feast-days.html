Sri Lankan Parish Feast Traditions

Step 1 • Flag Hoisting

Kodi Gaha Eswema

(Opening of 9-Day Novena)

The traditional wooden flagpole is decorated with areca-palm fronds, coloured banners, and elevated amid the ringing of church bells and hymn chanting to inaugurate the 9-day novena period.

Step 2 • 9-Day Novena

Navena Prarthana

Evenings of devout prayer, holy rosary, litany of the patron saint, homily by visiting preachers, and the solemn Benediction of the Blessed Sacrament for all parishioners.

Step 3 • Eve of the Feast

Vespers & Perahera

Chanted evening Vespers followed by the illuminated street palanquin procession carrying the sacred statue of the Saint through village lanes with brass bands, flower carpets, and fireworks.

Step 4 • Feast Day

Maha Mangallaya

Solemn High Festal Mass concelebrated by the Bishop or Parish Priest, special blessing over the sick and children, blessing of bread, and community hospitality (Kevili and tea fellowship).
