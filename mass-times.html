Church Information

Holy Rosary Church, the Golden Shrine of the Infant Jesus, is a renowned Catholic church in Slave Island, Colombo 02. The church is dedicated to the Infant Jesus and attracts many devotees throughout the year. It is a place of prayer, devotion and pilgrimage, where the faithful come to seek blessings and offer their intentions.
